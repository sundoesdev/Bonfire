It shows you cards ordered by how many times that one was reviewed, the top 3 cards you've studied the most, your top 3 "favorite" cards (which you can mark somewhere, possibly future ideas later when Hearth becomes more social, like leaderboard and online features.). This should give you a good idea though of what to expect as we push forward with Hearth development, specifically on the stats page.

- Earlier, in older versions of the software, we had the ability to mark cards for studying or not studying. I would like to bring this ability back to Hearth. To describe this in deeper detail, anytime we want to take a card out of the studying rotation (aka, it will no longer appear in future study sessions), we turn it off for review, where it is then added to a "Archived" deck. This will not remove it from any of its current decks, and all cards in Archive are still prone to the decay properties where if a card is not studied often or stays in Archived for a very long time, we just assume that the user forgot it and we do not rely on them to study it outside of the Hearth software. Most mental descisions regarding study times should all assume that: The user is NOT studying the same material outside of the software, therefore, we assume that the material in Hearth is decaying slowly the less times consistently review it.
